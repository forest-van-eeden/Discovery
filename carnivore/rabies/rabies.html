Rabies Theory

Note: This page is a speculative theory written by a student. It is not established science and not medical advice. Nothing here has been tested or peer reviewed, and it should not be used to diagnose or treat anything. If you are unwell, please see a clinician.

The idea

I theorize that diets built on raw or processed animal flesh might change how the body handles decaying matter, and that this could be linked to some of the symptoms we call "rabies". This is a speculative idea about diet and digestion, written as a way to ask questions, not a claim about how rabies works.

Part of my reasoning is an etymology. I speculate that "rabies" could be read as "raw" + "bies" (Dutch/Afrikaans words I take to mean "remains" and "body"). I want to be clear that dictionaries disagree: "rabies" comes from the Latin rabies, "madness" or "rage", from rabere "to rage". My reading is a hypothesis to check with a linguist, not an established origin.

What is already known

  • Rabies is caused by the rabies lyssavirus, spread mainly by the bite or saliva of an infected animal. It is almost always fatal once symptoms start, but it is preventable with vaccination and prompt post-exposure treatment.
  • Rabies is not caused by eating meat, by eating raw meat, or by diet. Vegans can get it from a bite, and meat eaters who are not bitten do not get it.
  • Other things I wrote about before, including a global outbreak, "paralyzing rabies", schizophrenia as a result of rabies, and cannibalism etymology, are not supported by any source I have, so I have removed them.

How the theory could be tested

A diet theory of rabies would predict that unbitten people on raw-meat diets show rabies-like illness and that the rabies virus is absent. No such cases are known. A study could look for any rabies-like illness in people with no animal exposure, and test for the virus. If the virus is always present, my diet theory does not hold.

Safety

If you are bitten or scratched by an animal that might have rabies, wash the wound well and contact a doctor or the local health department straight away. Please do not rely on diet, plants or home remedies. An earlier version of this page named plants to eat. I have removed them, because some of them are toxic.
