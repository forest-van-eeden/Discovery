Hormones Theory

Note: This page is a speculative theory written by a student. It is not established science and not medical advice. Nothing here has been tested or peer reviewed, and it should not be used to diagnose or treat anything. If you are unwell, please see a clinician.

The idea

I theorize that the balance of hormones between partners might matter for fertility, and I wonder whether taking outside hormones could change that balance. This is a speculative question. I do not have evidence for it, and it does not describe what happens in medicine.

What is already known

  • One person's hormone therapy does not change their partner's hormone levels, and it does not harm their partner.
  • Hormone therapy under medical supervision, including estrogen for transgender women, is an established treatment. It can reduce fertility, which is why clinicians discuss fertility preservation first.

How the theory could be tested

If partners' hormones were linked, partners' blood levels should change together when one starts a hormone. A small study measuring both partners over time would show whether they do. I expect they would not.
